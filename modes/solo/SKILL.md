# The solo loop

Step 9 writes this loop in full; what is here says what it will be. There is one
role and one turn: delegate `solo` at the project root with the brief as it
stands, `wait` for it, and read `result`. Nothing is branched, nothing is
merged, and no worktree exists — a solo task reads the project and reports, so a
question that needs no team costs one engine and no ceremony. When the answer
turns out to need a change to the repository, that is a `dev-team` task, not a
second solo turn.
